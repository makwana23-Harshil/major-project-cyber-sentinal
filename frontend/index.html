Core Modules

Everything You Need to
Stay Protected

Four powerful AI modules working together to give you the most comprehensive threat detection available.

SMS Spam Detection

ML-powered Naive Bayes classifier detects spam and scam SMS messages. Automatically extracts and inspects any URLs embedded in the message.

Email Address Validator

Checks syntax, MX DNS records, domain age via WHOIS, and flags disposable/temporary email providers. Gives a risk score for any address.

Email Body Analysis

SVM-based classifier identifies phishing and spam email bodies. Extracts all hyperlinks and runs each through deep inspection.

URL & Link Inspector

Crawls the live page, follows redirects, checks SSL, analyzes content for phishing patterns, brand impersonation, and checks VirusTotal + Google Safe Browsing APIs.
